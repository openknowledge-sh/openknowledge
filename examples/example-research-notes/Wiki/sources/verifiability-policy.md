# Wikipedia Verifiability policy

## Source record

- Publisher: English Wikipedia community
- Page type: Policy
- Reviewed: 2026-08-08
- Resource: [Wikipedia:Verifiability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Verifiability)

## Relevant points

- Readers must be able to check claims against reliable published sources.
- Challenged or likely challenged claims need an inline citation.
- The cited source must directly support the claim as presented.
- The editor who adds or restores material carries the burden of support.
