# Wikipedia Reliable sources guideline

## Source record

- Publisher: English Wikipedia community
- Page type: Content guideline
- Reviewed: 2026-08-08
- Resource: [Wikipedia:Reliable sources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources)

## Relevant points

- Reliability depends on the source and the claim context.
- The work, creator, publication, and publisher can affect reliability.
- Sources should directly support the material that cites them.
- A reliable source for one statement can be unsuitable for another.
