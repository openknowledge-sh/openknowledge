## Scope and mode

Operate-mode, self-contained HTML artifact emitted by `openknowledge quality
report --format html --out <file>`.

## Audience and job

Agent builders and knowledge owners need to identify the current bundle's
concrete risks, distinguish missing measurement from a healthy zero, and choose
the next knowledge path to inspect or maintain.

## Task, proof, and constraints

Lead with measurement completeness and the north-star contract, then an
actionable priority queue. Preserve every report metric, current-revision
binding, generation outcome, and eval change. Remain offline, keyboard
operable, responsive, printable, and useful with JavaScript disabled; filters
are progressive enhancement.

## Direction

Release ledger in the established Open Knowledge visual world: daylight
operational paper, ruled rows, restrained green, explicit risk labels. The
memorable moment is moving from the horizontal health ledger directly into a
filterable queue of exact knowledge paths without a global score.

## Unresolved

No durable hosted-dashboard navigation or real-time refresh behavior is
defined; this surface is an exported snapshot.
